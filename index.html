What is ODS (Orbit Dispatch System)?

Orbit Dispatch System (ODS) is a smart dispatch platform designed to manage trips, drivers, and fleet operations in real time. It helps automate routing, improve efficiency, and provide live tracking for transport and logistics services.

Built for modern UK-style dispatch operations, ODS brings everything into one system. from booking to completion with speed, accuracy, and reliability.

How Orbit Dispatch Makes Transportation Easier

ODS simplifies taxi and transport operations by automating bookings, dispatch, tracking, and payments — all in one platform.

Quick Booking

Customers can book rides instantly via app or website with automatic fare calculation.

Smart Driver Allocations

The system automatically assigns the nearest available driver to reduce waiting time.

Fast Driver Updates

Drivers receive trip details instantly on their app for fast response.

Live Current Tracking

Track every trip in real time for full visibility and control.

Safe Payments

Automated payment processing with multiple secure payment methods.
